Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

We advise clients on how to handle data responsibly, so we hold ourselves to the same standard. This policy explains what information Asena Security collects through this website, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect very little, and we never sell it.

01Who we are

Asena Security (“Asena,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a security consulting practice. This policy applies to our website at www.asenasecurity.com and to our blog at blog.asenasecurity.com (together, the “Site”).

02Information we collect

Information you send us. The Site has no contact forms, user accounts, or comment sections. If you email us, for example at info@asenasecurity.com, we receive whatever you choose to include: typically your name, email address, company, and details about your inquiry.

Information collected automatically. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this website. However, the services that deliver the Site may process limited technical data, as described in section 05.

03How we use your information

We use the information you send us to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule a briefing
  • Scope, propose, and deliver services you request
  • Keep business records and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

04Cookies

This website does not set cookies. If that changes, for example if we add a scheduling tool or analytics, we will update this policy first.

05Service providers and third-party content

We keep our vendor footprint small and evaluate each provider the way we would advise a client to. The Site currently relies on:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the Site’s typefaces. When your browser loads a font, Google receives your IP address and browser details. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our email provider, which stores the messages you send us.

Blog posts may occasionally embed content from other sites, such as code snippets or videos. Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the other site directly, and that site may collect data about you under its own policies.

06Client engagement data

Information you share with us during a paid engagement, such as system details, policies, audit evidence, or source code, is not covered by this website policy. It is governed by the confidentiality and data-handling terms in your Statement of Work or Services Agreement.

07How long we keep it

We keep email correspondence for as long as we need it to respond to you, deliver services, and meet our record-keeping obligations. After that, we delete it.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain uses of it. You can make any of these requests by emailing info@asenasecurity.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. We may keep information we are legally required to retain.

09Security

We protect the information you send us with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including multi-factor authentication and encryption where available. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take the responsibility seriously. It is the work we do for a living.

10Children

The Site is intended for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site or our practices change. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

12Contact

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data? Email info@asenasecurity.com.
